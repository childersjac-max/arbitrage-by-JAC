## DELIVERABLE

Professional arbitrage dashboard with:

### A) Split-leg opportunity cards
- Header: sport badge, event, market label, time, green `+$profit • X% ROI` pill
- Two leg columns: book, selection+line, american odds + market avg, stake $, payout, Bet First
- Place Both button
- Comparison table: all books, best odds highlighted green

### B) Filters panel (reference UI — screenshot)
- **Wager Amount** — `$` input default `1000.00` (recalculates all card stakes live; optional bankroll slider $100–$50k may sync with this)
- **Sort By** — radio: `ROI %` (default) | `Profit $`
- **Sportsbooks** — collapsible multi-select, badge e.g. `35 Selected`
- **Sports & Leagues** — grouped by sport, badge e.g. `44 Selected`
- **Market Type** — scrollable multi-select (100+ types), badge e.g. `1028 Selected`
- Footer: **Reset all** | **Save Preset** | **Apply Filters** (primary blue)

Filters apply client-side to `opportunities[]` from `GET /api/status?day=today`.
